FAQ

Questions buyers and drivers ask before getting started.

Do I need an OBD-II adapter?

Yes. Diagnose AI needs a compatible OBD-II adapter to read fault codes and vehicle data from your car. The adapter plugs into the vehicle's diagnostic port.

Can Diagnose AI tell me whether a used car is safe to buy?

No app can guarantee a vehicle's condition. Diagnose AI helps you identify supported diagnostic signals, organize what they may mean, and decide when to ask more questions or get a qualified pre-purchase inspection.

Does a recent reset clue prove the seller is hiding something?

No. Incomplete readiness monitors or recently cleared information can happen for many reasons. They are useful context to ask about and, if needed, investigate with a mechanic—not proof of dishonesty or mileage fraud.

What does the buyer report include?

Where supported, the report can organize fault codes, readiness status, reset context, VIN consistency, applicable recall information, live data, priorities, and practical questions to ask the seller.

Is this a replacement for a mechanic?

No. Diagnose AI provides informational guidance to help you understand codes and prepare for repair. Always verify safety-critical issues with a qualified mechanic.

Can the app tell me if it is safe to drive?

The app can provide practical urgency guidance based on the code, common causes, and symptoms. It cannot guarantee safety. If the vehicle feels unsafe, stop driving and get professional help.

Does every vehicle show the same live data?

No. Available data depends on the vehicle, adapter, ECU support, and connection quality. Some vehicles provide more sensor data than others.

How do I restore a Pro subscription?

Open the app, go to your profile or subscription screen, and tap Restore Purchases. App Store purchases are managed by Apple.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026. Last Updated: October 17, 2026.

Diagnose AI is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Your email address and account identifier are used for authentication, account management, and access to your saved data and purchases through Supabase Auth. Passwords are not stored in plaintext.
  • Vehicle and VIN Data: Vehicle year, make, model, engine, mileage, and other details you provide. A VIN entered for Quick Check is sent to our backend and NHTSA to identify the vehicle. The Quick Check record stores a VIN hash and the last six characters, alongside vehicle details and matched recall information.
  • Diagnostic and Buyer Report Data: Fault codes, available vehicle readings, symptoms, guided-check answers, and generated reports may be stored with your account so you can reopen them. Exported PDFs may contain vehicle details and your findings; you control where you save or share them.
  • AI Chat: Your messages, relevant conversation history, and vehicle or diagnostic context are processed to answer your questions. Avoid entering personal or sensitive information in symptom descriptions or chat messages.
  • Purchase Data: Store transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, and an account identifier are used to verify subscriptions and Buyer Report purchases. Apple or Google processes payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Usage and Diagnostics: When analytics is enabled, limited app-interaction events and redacted error information help us understand reliability and feature use. Analytics uses pseudonymous identifiers, not your email, VIN, report contents, or chat text. Session replay is disabled.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account and display your vehicles, scan history, and saved Buyer Reports.
  • To identify vehicles, look up matched recalls, and turn diagnostic data or guided checks into informational guidance.
  • To provide AI diagnostics and chat responses using the vehicle context and messages needed for your request.
  • To verify and manage subscriptions and separate Buyer Report purchases through Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat.
  • To maintain security, respond to support requests, and improve app reliability.

3. AI Processing

When you use AI diagnostics or chat, relevant fault codes, vehicle details, readings, symptoms, prior analysis, chat messages, and conversation history are sent to Google Gemini for processing. We do not intentionally add your account email, name, or user ID to AI prompts, but information you type into free-text fields may be included. The app presents an AI-processing disclosure before use. Your data is not sold for advertising.

4. Data Security

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Supabase Row Level Security and backend authorization checks restrict access to account-linked data. Our backend is hosted on Amazon Web Services.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, and Row Level Security.
  • Amazon Web Services: Backend hosting and request processing.
  • Google Gemini AI: Processing of diagnostic context and AI chat messages.
  • NHTSA: VIN decoding and vehicle recall lookup.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and purchase management.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: Store payments, subscriptions, and purchase records.
  • PostHog, when enabled: Limited pseudonymous usage events and redacted error diagnostics.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account-linked vehicle, scan, and saved-report data is retained to provide those features until you delete it or your account. You can request account deletion in the app or by emailing team@diagnoseai.us. Email requests are handled within 30 days unless retention is required by law. Some transaction, fraud-prevention, or service-provider records may be retained where necessary. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription: manage cancellation in the store that processed your purchase. PDFs you have exported to your device or shared elsewhere are not removed by deleting your account.
